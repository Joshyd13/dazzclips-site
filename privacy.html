Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

DazzClips Uploader is a private desktop tool used only by its owner on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos on the owner's YouTube channels and to read the public statistics (views and likes) of those videos.

What it stores

The sign-in token and a list of the uploaded videos are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, or shared with anyone.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect, store or process data about any other person. Nobody other than the owner can sign in.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's folder deletes everything it stored.

Google policies

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, and the tool uses YouTube API Services under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
